Find Us

Come visit our campus

We are located in Karen, Nairobi — five forested acres, minutes from Karura Forest.

Langata South Road, Karen, Nairobi

By Car

From Nairobi CBD, take Langata Road towards Karen. Turn onto Langata South Road and look for our entrance 200m past the Karen Country Club turn-off. Parking is available on campus.

By Matatu

Take Route 24 or Route 125 to the Karen Hub. From there, it is a 10-minute walk, or a short boda boda ride down Langata South Road.

School Transport

We operate school transport routes across Nairobi — including Karen, Langata, Westlands, Kilimani, and Runda. Contact our operations team for routes and fees.
