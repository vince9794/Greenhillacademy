A Word from Admissions

Karibuni to Greenhill.

Thank you for considering The Greenhill School for your child. Choosing a school is one of the most important decisions a family makes — and we want to make that decision as clear, warm, and unhurried as possible.

Our admissions process is designed to help us know your child, not just their file. We want to understand who they are, what they love, and what they need — so that we can be the right place for them.

Whether you are moving to Nairobi from another city or country, or you are a Nairobi family seeking something different, we would love to meet you. Karibu sana.

Application Process

Four simple steps

Our process is transparent, personal, and unhurried. Here is exactly what to expect.

1

Submit Enquiry

Reach out via our online form, email, or phone. We'll respond within two working days.

2

Campus Visit & Assessment

Visit our Karen campus, meet our teachers, and let your child spend a morning with us.

3

Interview & Offer

We meet with your family to discuss placement. Successful families receive an offer letter.

4

Enrolment & Orientation

Complete enrolment, pay fees, and join our family orientation before the new term.

Requirements

What you'll need to apply

Here is what we ask every family to prepare. If anything is difficult to source, please let us know — we can help.

Required Documents

These documents help us understand your child's journey so far. Please bring originals and copies to your campus visit.

  • Birth certificate (original + copy)
  • Passport photos (4 recent, passport-size)
  • Previous school reports (last 2 years)
  • Transfer letter (if moving from another school)
  • Immunisation records (from your paediatrician)
  • Medical form (completed by a doctor)
  • Parent/guardian ID (or passport for international families)

Assessment & Interview

Our assessment is designed to be warm and unhurried — it helps us understand your child's strengths, interests, and learning style.

  • Age-appropriate assessment — literacy, numeracy, and reasoning (Grades 1–8)
  • Play-based observation — for Early Years applicants
  • Interview with parents — to discuss your family's hopes and questions
  • Pastoral reference — from a previous school (if applicable)
  • Special needs disclosure — so we can prepare the right support
  • No exam prep required — please come as you are

Scholarships & Bursaries

We don't turn away curious children.

The Need-Based Bursary

The Greenhill Scholarship

For children who demonstrate a deep connection to nature, community service, or environmental stewardship.

Up to 40% of fees

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions families often ask

If your question isn't answered here, please reach out — we're always happy to help.

We accept applications year-round, but places fill quickly for the following academic year. We recommend enquiring at least 6–9 months before your preferred start date, especially for popular grades. Mid-year admissions are considered on a case-by-case basis where space allows.

Yes. We operate school transport routes across Karen, Langata, Westlands, Lavington, Kilimani, and Kileleshwa. Routes are confirmed each term based on demand. Contact our operations team for current routes and fees.

Greenhill is a non-denominational school. We welcome families of all faiths and none. Our values program draws on universal principles of kindness, integrity, and community, and we hold a moment of quiet reflection at the start of each week.

We follow Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) from PP1 through Grade 8, including the national KPSEA (Grade 6) and KJSEA (Grade 9) assessments. Our progressive pedagogy integrates child-led, Waldorf-inspired, and nature-based practices with the national framework.

Our average class size is 18 students, with an 8:1 student-to-teacher ratio. We cap classes at 20 to ensure every child is known and supported as an individual.

Greenhill is a day school. We do not offer boarding, though we do offer after-school programs and holiday camps. For families moving to Nairobi from outside the city, we're happy to recommend trusted guardianship options near the school.

We are an inclusive school and welcome children with a range of learning profiles. Our learning support team works closely with families, and we can provide in-class support and, where needed, individual sessions. We ask that you share any special needs during the admissions process so we can prepare the right support from day one.
