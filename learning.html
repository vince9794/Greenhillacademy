Academics at Greenhill

Learning that honors childhood.

At Greenhill, academics are not separate from childhood — they are woven through it. Our curriculum is rigorous, but it is delivered at the pace of a child, not the pace of a syllabus.

Home / Academics
Our Approach

A curriculum that follows the child.

Our academic approach is built on a simple belief: children learn best when learning is unhurried, connected to the real world, and driven by their own curiosity. We do not rush children through content. We go deep, we go slow, and we go together.

Every day at Greenhill blends focused academic work with time outdoors, time for creative expression, and time for the kind of unstructured play that builds character and imagination. Our teachers are experts in their fields — but more importantly, they are experts in children.

Slow, deep learning

We cover fewer topics, but we cover them properly. Depth over breadth, always.

Nature-based

Our five-acre forest campus is a classroom. Learning happens outside as much as inside.

Known by name

With an 8:1 student-teacher ratio, every child is known, seen, and supported as an individual.

Curriculum

What we teach — and why.

Greenhill is a Kenyan school, and we are proud to follow the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) — the national framework that places competencies, values, and real-world skills at the center of learning. We integrate CBC with our own progressive pedagogy, drawing on child-led, Waldorf-inspired, and nature-based practices.

Our curriculum is organized around subjects, but the learning is organized around the child. Lessons are cross-curricular, project-based, and grounded in the world children actually live in.

Aligned with Kenya's CBC Greenhill's academic program follows the CBC from PP1 through Grade 8, including the national assessments at Grade 6 (KPSEA) and Grade 9 (KJSEA). Our students are well prepared for both national benchmarks and international pathways.

Ages & Stages

Learning through the years

Our program grows with the child — from the play-based years through to the challenges of middle childhood.

Ages 4–6

Early Years

Play-based, unhurried, and full of story. Children explore the world through the senses — sand, water, paint, garden, and song.

Grades 1–3

Lower Primary

Foundations of literacy and numeracy, taught through story, art, and the natural world. Children begin to read, write, and reason.

Grades 7–8

Assessment

How we know children are learning

We assess children through observation, portfolios, and conversation — not just exams.

Continuous, Observational Assessment

Our teachers observe children every day — not to grade them, but to understand them. Each child has a personal portfolio, updated termly with work, projects, and reflections.

  • Portfolios — a living record of the child's learning.
  • Teacher observation — how children engage, question, and collaborate.
  • Projects & presentations — real work that shows real understanding.
  • Parent conferences — twice-yearly, unhurried conversations.

National Assessments

As a Kenyan school, we participate in the national assessments required by the Ministry of Education and the Kenya National Examinations Council (KNEC). Our children are prepared without being reduced to test scores.

  • KPSEA (Grade 6) — Kenya Primary School Education Assessment.
  • KJSEA (Grade 9) — Kenya Junior Secondary Education Assessment.
  • CBC competencies — integrated throughout every year group.
  • Teacher-prepared mocks — gently introduced from Grade 5.
