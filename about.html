About Greenhill

A school built on curiosity and care.

Since 2016, The Greenhill School has offered Nairobi families a different kind of education — one that honours childhood, respects nature, and trusts every child's natural curiosity.

Our Story

From a small circle of parents to a thriving community.

Greenhill began in a living room in Karen in 2016 — a small group of Kenyan educators and parents who shared one conviction: that childhood should not be hurried, and that a school could honour both academic depth and the slow unfolding of a child's mind.

We opened our doors with 24 children, three teachers, and a borrowed garden. Within two years, we had planted the first trees on our own five-acre forested campus — trees that today shade our classrooms, our play areas, and our reading nooks.

Nearly a decade later, Greenhill is home to more than 500 children from across Nairobi. Our commitment has not changed: we follow the child, we follow the seasons, and we follow curiosity.

Our Values

What we stand for

Four values shape everything we do — from how we teach to how we greet each other at the gate.

I

Curiosity

We trust the child's natural instinct to ask, explore, and wonder. Learning begins with a question, not an answer.

II

Care

Every child is known by name. We tend to one another — classmates, teachers, parents, and the land we learn on.

III

Community

Greenhill is a village. Parents are partners, teachers are mentors, and children learn what it means to belong.

IV

Courage

We encourage children to try, to fail gently, and to try again. Courage grows when it is welcomed, not punished.

Governance

How Greenhill is governed.

Greenhill is an independent school, governed by a voluntary Board of Governors and supported by an active Parents' Association and an Alumni Council.

The Board meets quarterly and is responsible for strategy, financial oversight, and the appointment of the Head of School. The Parents' Association and Alumni Council provide community voice, fundraising, and volunteer support.

Our governance reflects our values: transparency, community, and a long-term view of what is best for the children in our care.

Our Crest

The symbols behind our school.

Our crest was designed by our founding class in 2016. It carries the symbols of our school — the hill for which we are named, the sun of curiosity, the open book of learning, and the motto that has guided us since the beginning.

The Hill

A rising hill, echoing the Ngong Hills visible from our campus, and the gentle climb of learning.

The Sun

Curiosity as a light — rising each day, illuminating what we did not know yesterday.

The Open Book

The books, stories, and ideas that shape our learning — always open, always inviting.

Ad Astra Per Curiositatem

To the stars through curiosity — our motto, guiding every child who passes through our gates.
