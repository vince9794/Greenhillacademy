A Word from Our Head

Karibuni to Greenhill.

When we founded Greenhill in 2016, we began with a simple question: what if a school could honour both academic excellence and the unhurried wonder of childhood? What if our children could learn mathematics under a fig tree, or study history by walking through the Ngong Hills?

Nearly a decade later, that question has become our answer. Our five-acre forested campus in the heart of Karen — minutes from Karura Forest — is a place where curiosity is the curriculum, where kindness is a discipline, and where every child is known by name.

We invite you to visit us, sit under our trees, and see what a Greenhill education feels like. Karibuni sana.

Grace Wanjiru
Head of School · The Greenhill School
